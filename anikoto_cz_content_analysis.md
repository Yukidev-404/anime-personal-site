### 3.2 Fantasy Genre
- **Strong presence** throughout catalog
- Includes: Isekai, Magic, Supernatural
- Popular isekai titles: Overgeared, Reincarnated Aristocrat, Goblin
- Often combined with Action or Romance

### 3.3 Romance Genre
- **Significant category** with dedicated filtering
- Includes: Harem, Shoujo Ai, Josei, School romance
- Popular titles: Firefly Wedding, various romance-focused series
- Often combined with Comedy or Harem elements

### 3.4 Isekai Genre
- **Rapidly growing category**
- Common premise: Protagonist transported to another world
- Recent additions: Overgeared, Full Clearing Another World, Goblin Rebirth
- Often features game-like elements or fantasy settings

### 3.5 Harem/Ecchi Genre
- **Dedicated category** with hentai subgenre
- Includes: Harem, Ecchi, Erotica
- Popular titles feature male protagonist with multiple female interests
- Distinct section separate from mainstream romance

### 3.6 Slice of Life Genre
- **Present but smaller category**
- Everyday life settings, character-focused
- Less action-driven, more character development
- Often combined with Romance or Comedy

### 3.7 Sports Genre
- **Niche but present category**

- Smaller selection compared to Action/Fantasy

### 3.8 Horror/Supernatural Genre
- **Present category** with dedicated navigation
